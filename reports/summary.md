## Time Series Insights

- **Long‑Term Upward Trend**  
  The trend component shows a clear, steady increase in average new car prices from early 2019 (~\$25 000) to late 2023 (~\$33 000), reflecting persistent inflationary pressure and supply‑chain constraints.

- **Seasonal Fluctuations**  
  There’s a robust annual cycle: prices tend to dip every January–February and peak around June–July. This likely corresponds to model‑year rollovers and summer buying incentives.

- **Residual Noise**  
  The remainder series highlights occasional sharp deviations—e.g., mid‑2020 spikes—coinciding with COVID‑related shortages and stimulus‑driven demand surges.

- **12‑Month Forecast**  
  The ARIMA(1,1,1) model projects continued growth through 2025, with the 95 % confidence interval spanning roughly \$32 000–\$36 000 by mid‑2025. This suggests dealers and buyers should plan for further price increases.
